How Does Visiocore Vision Support Work?

Vision supplements often work by providing nutrients that support normal eye structure and function. For example, vitamin A is needed for the visual cycle and zinc is used in a number of processes that contribute to normal vision. Vitamins C and E are among the body's antioxidant systems that help guard cells against oxidative damage. These nutrients are important, but eating more of them won’t necessarily improve the vision of someone who already gets enough from food.

Some eye health formulas include lutein and zeaxanthin, carotenoids found in the retina, especially in the macula. These compounds have been investigated for age related macular degeneration. In the Age-Related Eye Disease Study 2 (AREDS2), researchers found that a specific combination of nutrients may benefit some people with intermediate age-related macular degeneration or advanced disease in one eye. This evidence is specific to a certain formulation and group of patients, and does not apply to all products advertized for vision support.

Visiocore Vision Support Side Effects & Risks

Dietary supplements can cause side effects depending on ingredients, dosage and health of user. Some formulations can cause nausea, upset stomach, constipation or other digestive symptoms. Some vitamins and minerals can also be harmful in high doses. A verified list of ingredients and dosages is required to assign a reliable product-specific safety profile for Visiocore Vision Support.
